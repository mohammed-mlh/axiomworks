Privacy Policy for VibeTest

Effective date: June 21, 2026

VibeTest ("the App") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the App, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, VibeTest may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address or sign-in details, if you create an account.
  • App usage information, such as quiz activity, selections, session data, and basic analytics.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system, app version, language, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Advertising or monetization data, if ads are shown in the App.
  • Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID/GAID and Apple ID for Advertisers/IDFA) and related ad interaction data may be collected by third-party advertising partners.

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the App:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics Privacy Policy

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the App.
  • Save your progress and improve quiz and app features.
  • Analyze performance, fix bugs, and improve stability.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and enforce policies.
  • Show ads or measure ad performance, if applicable.
  • Serve ads through third-party providers such as Google AdMob.

Data Sharing

VibeTest does not sell your personal data.

Information may be shared only when needed with:

  • Service providers that help operate the App, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, crash reporting, or advertising providers including Google AdMob.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data Retention

Data is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Choices

Depending on your location and how you use the App, you may be able to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your data.
  • Stop using the App at any time.
  • Adjust device settings related to permissions, tracking, or ads.

Children

VibeTest is not intended for children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us to request review or deletion.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at the same public URL with a revised effective date.
